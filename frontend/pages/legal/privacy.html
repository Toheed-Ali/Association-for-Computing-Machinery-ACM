This Privacy Policy explains how the ACM ITU Student Chapter ("ACM ITU", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information we receive through this Website. By visiting and/or contacting us through the Website, you acknowledge that we may collect, use, and transfer your Personal Information as described in this Policy.
01

How We Collect & Use Your Information

ACM ITU only collects Personal Information that is relevant to its activities and seeks to ensure that it is accurate and up to date. Personal Information we may collect includes your name, email address, phone number, university department, and year of study.

We may use your Personal Information to:

  • Correspond and stay in touch with you regarding ACM ITU activities, events, and updates.
  • Process your membership registration and manage your account.
  • Process your registration for events, workshops, competitions, or other activities.
  • Improve our Website and community offerings to better serve members.
  • Comply with our legal obligations.
  • Evaluate diversity, equity, and inclusion across ACM ITU activities as part of our commitment to a welcoming community.

We will only process your personal data where we have a legal basis for doing so, including to fulfill our agreement with you, to pursue our legitimate interests, to comply with applicable law, or where we have your consent.

Member Databases

ACM ITU maintains member databases containing mailing and profile information such as your name, contact details, and membership record. This information is used by authorized ACM ITU staff to process registrations, send event announcements, respond to inquiries, and improve our offerings. Member records are maintained for as long as an individual is an active member and for two years following a membership lapse.

All personally identifiable information in ACM ITU membership and registration databases is treated as confidential and used only for the purposes stated in this Policy.

ACM ITU Emails

From time to time, ACM ITU may send emails to members or visitors, such as:

  • Service messages including registration confirmations, renewal notices, and event reminders.
  • Announcements about ACM ITU activities, workshops, and opportunities we believe are relevant to you.
  • Emails related to specific programs or events for which you have registered.

ACM ITU does not sell, rent, or exchange email addresses of its members or users. If you wish to unsubscribe from any emails, you may do so by following the unsubscribe instructions at the bottom of each email or by contacting us at ituxacm@gmail.com.

Web Accounts

To create an account on the ACM ITU Website, you will be asked to provide your name, university email address, and create a password. This information is used to manage your account and provide access to member-only features. ACM ITU may send information about activities and services to account holders via email. You may opt out at any time.

02

Technical Personal Information

ACM ITU may keep access logs containing information such as a visitor's IP address and search queries. We may analyze log files periodically to help maintain and improve our Website and enforce our online service policies. Raw log files are treated as confidential.

ACM ITU only uses cookies as necessary to operate the Website and does not use user-specific targeting cookies. Information collected about web account usage will be stored in an anonymized form and used to improve our Website and services.

03

Third Parties

From time to time, we may share Personal Information with third parties where:

  • We have your explicit consent to do so.
  • It is necessary for the provision of services you have requested.
  • It is required to process your registration for an event or activity.
  • It is required in response to valid legal process.
  • It is in our legitimate business interests.

Third parties with whom your Personal Information may be shared include outside contractors, event platforms, and consultants engaged to assist ACM ITU. We inform these third parties about safeguarding personal information and require them to use it only for an authorized purpose consistent with this Policy.

04

Global Transfer of Personal Information

ACM ITU is based at Information Technology University, Lahore, Pakistan. Personal information you provide may be transferred to and stored on servers operated by ACM ITU or its partners. Regardless of where your Personal Information is stored, we take all appropriate steps to protect it from unauthorized access, unlawful processing, accidental loss, destruction, and damage.

By submitting your Personal Information, you acknowledge this potential transfer, storage, and processing.

05

Links

Our Website may, from time to time, contain links to and from the websites of third parties. If you follow a link to any of these websites, please note that these websites have their own privacy policies and we do not accept any responsibility or liability for those policies. Please check these policies before you submit any Personal Information to those websites.

06

Security

The security of personal information is important to ACM ITU. We maintain all personal data with technical, administrative, and physical safeguards to protect against loss, unauthorized access, destruction, misuse, modification, and improper disclosure.

No computer system or information can ever be fully protected against every possible hazard. ACM ITU provides reasonable and appropriate security controls to protect personal information against foreseeable hazards.
07

Changes to this Policy

Any changes we may make to this Policy in the future will be posted on our Website. It is your responsibility to ensure that you are aware of the latest version of this Policy. By continuing to use our Website or otherwise engage with us, you will be deemed to have accepted any revised terms.

08

Your Rights

Subject to applicable data protection laws, you have the following rights in relation to the Personal Information we hold about you:

  • The right to request that we erase your Personal Information, which we will comply with unless we have a lawful reason to retain it.
  • The right to ask us to restrict or stop processing your Personal Information.
  • The right to request access to the Personal Information we hold about you.
  • The right to correct any inaccurate Personal Information we hold about you.
  • The right to withdraw consent to processing at any time, where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at ituxacm@gmail.com.

09

Updating Your Information

You can find out what Personal Information ACM ITU holds about you by contacting us at ituxacm@gmail.com. If any of the Personal Information we hold about you is incorrect, you can ask us to update or remove it by contacting us at the same address.

ACM ITU members may access and update their profile information by logging into their account on the Website.

10

Retention of Personal Information

We will only retain your Personal Information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes we collected it for, including satisfying any legal, regulatory, or reporting requirements.

To determine the appropriate retention period, we consider the amount, nature, and sensitivity of the Personal Information, the potential risk of harm from unauthorized use or disclosure, the purposes for which we process the data, and the applicable legal requirements.
